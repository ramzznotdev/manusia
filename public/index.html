Endpoint Tersedia

📱 OrderKouta

POST /api/orderkouta/get-otp Request kode OTP
POST /api/orderkouta/verify-otp Verifikasi OTP + Login
GET /api/orderkouta/mutasi Ambil riwayat mutasi
GET /api/orderkouta/profile Info akun & saldo

💳 Pakasir

POST /api/pakasir/create Buat transaksi baru
GET /api/pakasir/check Cek status transaksi
POST /api/pakasir/cancel Batalkan transaksi
